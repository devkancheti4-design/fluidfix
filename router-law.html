The Router Law

incident 1

A failing test lands on a large repository. Nothing reads the code to decide where to go: the lines the failing test executed are matched against the fault vocabulary, the matches become an integer, and the router law turns that integer into a dispatch. The guards that own those kinds search in parallel; the judge rules with one more integer. Every decision a model would charge for is arithmetic here.
