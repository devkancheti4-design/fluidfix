fluidfix · measured, not asserted

Two fixes. Both pass your tests.
One of them is wrong.

A passing suite says two patches agree on the handful of inputs somebody wrote down. It does not say they are the same program. When the tests cannot tell two candidates apart, the honest move is not to pick one — it is to go and find the input that separates them.

what it does today

  
Six gates. Nothing ships unless all six hold.

  
A green test run is one of the six, and on its own it is the weakest. Every gate below is
  measured on real patches, most of them written by models rather than by us.


  

    
RED BEFORE
      
The suite must actually reject the code as given.

      otherwise there is nothing to certify

    
GREEN AFTER
      
The full suite, never the failing test alone.

      caught 9 of 9 bad patches

    
STABLE
      
The green must repeat on independent re-runs.

      caught 14 of 14 intermittent patches

    
NO COLLATERAL
      
Every test green before is still green after.

      caught 6 of 6

    
UNIQUE
      
No rival patch that also passes but behaves differently.

      separated 14 of 14 rivals

    
ROLLBACK
      
On any refusal the tree is byte-identical to before.

      sha256 checked, every refusal

  


  

    
14/14
model-written fixes certified

      
Whole-function rewrites with inserted imports — nothing the tool could have authored itself.

    
29/29
bad patches refused

      
Wrong, intermittent, or breaking something else. Byte-exact rollback every time.

    
0
wrong repairs, ever

      
Across 240 generated bugs, 22 real-repo mutants and 14 model-written faults.

The rule that makes it safe is the same
  rule that makes it credulous: your suite is the only judge. It cannot see a behaviour
  nobody wrote a test for, and it will serve a suite that lies.










  
what a check costs

  
Zero tokens. The bill is suite runs.

  

    
4
suite runs — a certificate

      
One to establish red, one for green, two re-checks.

    
3
suite runs — a refusal

      
And it prints what it tried and which test killed each candidate.

    
5.2s
to find a known fault shape

      
5 nodes of 23. Cold, before it has learned anything: 22 nodes, 19.3s.

  

  
On one real handoff — searching 24 nodes,
  localising to one file of eight, certifying two patches and separating them — the tool spent
  0 tokens and 19 suite runs. The two models writing the fix it could not write
  spent ~96,000 tokens.










  
what we could grow into

  
Everything below this line is unmeasured

  

    
None of the following has been measured. It is what the mechanism permits, with the
    specific thing that would have to be true written under each one. We have kept it separate from the
    page above on purpose — the measurements are the only reason to believe any of it.

  


  

    
A gate on every merge

      
The same six checks run against a pull request instead of a local tree: no patch merges unless red
      became green, stayed green, broke nothing, and has no rival that also passes.

      needs: a CI integration, and a measured false-refusal rate on real PRs


    
Self-healing CI

      
A red build repairs itself where the fault is mechanical, and where it is not, opens an issue that
      already names the file, the classes eliminated, and the test that killed each candidate.

      needs: shape coverage of real incidents — the number we do not have


    
Learning from one team's history

      
Point the budget ladder at a year of resolved failures; the kinds that recur become taught classes.
      Two classes taught this way took the free tier from 1 in 14 to 5 in 14.

      needs: a real incident history, not a corpus we generated


    
A verifier for agent-written code

      
The judge does not ask who wrote the patch. An agent proposes, this decides, and nothing merges on
      the agent's own say-so.

      needs: proof the localisation makes the model cheaper — untested

  


  
The honest next step is not another
  benchmark. It is one repository, in report-only mode, for a week: let it watch real failures and sort them
  into kinds. That produces the one number none of our runs can — what fraction of
  incidents that actually happen are mechanical — and everything in this section depends on it.






  
Both demo cases are recorded runs. The drifted bound: boundary located exactly in 12 of 12 trials,
  where a fixed pool of suite-derived inputs found 0 of 12. The identical pair: 76 inputs probed, no witness,
  ruling withdrawn — and verified by hand to be one program spelled twice.
